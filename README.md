## About

Aerospace engineering student at Georgia Tech. Work in autonomous aircraft, flight controls, embedded computer vision, experimental research, manufacturing, and STEM education.

## Experience

**Autonomous systems**  
Integrated and flight-tested UAV systems with ArduPilot, RTK GPS, MAVLink, and CAN avionics.

**Embedded vision**  
Programmed AprilTag pose estimation on an Arduino Nicla Vision. Connected it to a Cube Orange+ flight controller.

**Experimental engineering**  
Designed biomechanics test hardware. Collected and analyzed torque, force, and displacement data in MATLAB.

**STEM education**  
Founded RAYSE-MN. Mentor students in robotics, controls, CAD, testing, and system integration.

## Current work

**Yellow Jacket Space Program**  
Flight controls, six degree of freedom simulation, fault injection, and FDIR testing.

**Georgia Tech Supersonics**  
Propane feed system for an afterburner test stand torch igniter.

**Autonomous omnicopter**  
Eight reversible thrust rotors, a carbon tube frame, and printed structural joints.
